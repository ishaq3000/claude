## Inspect: answers

- **What is it?** A head-to-head of two ~300 Wh portable power stations: the Jackery Explorer 300 (Amazon B082TMBYR6) and the Anker SOLIX C300 DC (Amazon B0D62PMB3R).
- **Who is it for?** Campers, road-trippers, van/car travelers, and people building a small blackout kit, trying to pick one of the two.
- **What sets them apart?** Same capacity, opposite philosophies: the Jackery has **real AC outlets**. The Anker is **DC/USB-C only**, but has a LiFePO4 battery (3000 vs 500 cycles), 2×140 W USB-C, ~1 h recharge, and half the volume.
- **Most impressive claim:** 6× the rated battery lifespan at about the same capacity.
- **Visual hook:** two illustrated units slam in from opposite sides, then "293 Wh vs 288 Wh", with an "AC vs USB-C" split.
- **Real material:** Amazon and the brand sites are blocked by this environment's network policy, so the products are drawn as vector illustrations from their published shapes. All numbers are the makers' published specs. Runtimes are labeled as estimates.
- **Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Angle

"Almost identical on paper, completely different in practice." One round (AC output) can decide it on its own, so the verdict is conditional: **need wall-plug AC, get the Jackery; live on USB-C, get the Anker.**
